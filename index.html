my-youtube

my-youtube is a personal automation tool used only by its developer to manage their own YouTube channel and Gmail account through the official Google APIs (YouTube Data API v3, YouTube Analytics API, Gmail API).

It uploads videos, edits video metadata, manages playlists and comments on the developer's own channel, and reads, sorts and sends email in the developer's own mailbox. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
